# DGX live accuracy: diagnosis and fixes, 2026-11-16/17

Core NEXA (DGX, DEV), live index `idx_20261110062959_4a9e3c21`, dataset 1.2.0, thresholds 1.4.0.
Tools: `v1/tools/nexa_diagnose.py` (read-only), `v1/tools/nexa_eval.py live`.

## Cause

On the curriculum files alone (corpus suite) the DGX scores as the school server does (gating recall@5
95.8). The live index also holds 4,576 chunks of EduNex LMS content scoped to `edunex-core-test`. The
diagnosis of 2026-11-16 (as platform_admin, on 9b6c06c) found that none of the 14 missed
questions was missing its answer: all were at rank 6-50. What held the top 5: LMS content 54%, the expected
source 40%, near copies 11%, another grade's content 7%. Causes: copies of a lesson split at different
places (containment 0.97, Jaccard below 0.8); Grade 11 Moodle lessons over the Grade 12 answer; the Physics
syllabus reference list; and the admin view, which sees the `edunex-core-test` content no other school's
learner sees.
